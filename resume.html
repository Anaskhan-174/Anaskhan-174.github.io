Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

  • Performed end-to-end analysis of 3,900 purchase records and 18 features using Python, Pandas, PostgreSQL, SQL and Power BI.
  • Cleaned and prepared the data by handling 37 missing Review Rating values, standardizing columns and creating analytical features such as age groups and purchase-frequency measures.

Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard

  • Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze listings, pricing, market performance, reviews, host performance and customer feedback across major global Airbnb markets.
  • Used Power Query for data preparation and DAX for KPI and analytical calculations, with dashboard views covering general overview, performance/pricing and reviews/customer feedback.
  • Compared major cities, property types and host performance, including Superhost benchmarking, average pricing, review ratings and host trust signals such as identity verification.
  • Identified that Paris, New York and Sydney account for almost half of total listings and 59% of total reviews, while hotel rooms had the highest average price among the displayed property types.
  • Found cleanliness and value for money as generally weaker rating dimensions and identified seasonal review patterns, supporting recommendations around pricing, market prioritization, customer experience and seasonal planning.
